Game 01 · Deepfakes, Voice Clones & Personalised Messages

Echo Chamber

Field Manual · Impersonation & Personalised Scale

  1. 01

    Voice cloning & deepfakes. A few seconds of a voice recording can be cloned to stage a family emergency, and AI video can make public figures or relatives appear to endorse investments or ask for money.

  2. 02

    Personalised at scale. AI writes thousands of texts, emails, and DMs using your location, hobbies, and basic details found online. A personal detail never proves a message is legitimate.

  3. 03

    The callback check. Never reply to a new number claiming to be family or a friend. Always take the time to call them back on the real number you already have saved.
