Field Manual · Mirror Websites & Fake Documentation

  1. 01

    Mirror websites in hours. AI copies logos, branding, contact forms, and live chat features within hours of a real service launching. Always type web addresses directly into your browser instead of clicking links in emails, messages, or ads.

  2. 02

    Fake documents in minutes. Investment scammers generate fake regulatory certificates; romance scammers generate fake passports and work permits. A polished PDF is never proof.

  3. 03

    Official lookup check. Verify any organisation that calls you by calling them back on a number you find yourself on their official website or app.
