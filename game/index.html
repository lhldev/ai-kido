Game #01 · /game/impersonation

Playable now

Echo Chamber: The Impersonation Desk

Scammers use AI to create fake videos, clone voices from a few seconds of audio, and send thousands of personalised messages using basic details found online. Inspect incoming calls, video clips, and texts — and verify before you act.

Threats covered
Voice cloning, deepfake technology & personalised messaging at scale
Golden rule
Call back on the real number you already have. Personal details do not prove legitimacy.

5 interactive rounds inside

  1. 01 Distress Voice Clone New number, cloned voice note in distress, urgent transfer
  2. 02 Personalised Message AI-scraped location & hobby details used to fake familiarity
  3. 03 Deepfake Endorsement Manipulated celebrity/public figure video pushing an investment
  4. 04 Family Video Call Cousin asking for money via a brief, scripted AI video clip
  5. 05 Scale Spear-Phishing Personalised competition DM vs. independent phone verification

Served at /game/impersonation · includes cloned voice audio samples.

Game #02 · /game/forgeries

Playable now

Paper Trail: Mirror Sites & Fake Docs

Scammers use AI tools to launch pixel-perfect copies of real websites within hours and generate official-looking regulatory certificates, passports, and work permits in minutes. Audit the URLs, registries, and documents to catch the forgeries.

Threats covered
Mirror websites, fake investment certificates & romance scam permits
Golden rule
Type web addresses directly into your browser, and verify callers using numbers from official websites or apps.

5 interactive rounds inside

  1. 01 Instant Mirror Website Same logo & working live chat, launched hours after a real service
  2. 02 Investment Certificate AI-generated regulatory licence with invented approval codes
  3. 03 Romance Visa & Permit Forged passport & work permit demanding a customs release fee
  4. 04 Sponsored Ad Mirror Social media advertisement link vs. typing the URL directly
  5. 05 Official Caller & Letter Fake compliance PDF + verifying an organisation via its official app

Served at /game/forgeries · standalone document & URL inspector.
