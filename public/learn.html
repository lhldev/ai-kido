  1. 01

    The introduction

    01 UNDERSTAND

    What Is an AI Scam?

    An AI scam is a trick that uses artificial intelligence to make fake messages, voices, images, or videos seem real. Scammers use these tools to gain your trust and persuade you to share personal information, reveal security codes, or send money.

    02 SEE

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. A message or video seems surprisingly convincing, even though its source is unfamiliar.
    2. Someone creates pressure or urgency to make you act without checking.
    3. You are asked to share private information, click an unfamiliar link, or send money.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Pause, verify the source independently, and never share sensitive information under pressure.

  2. 02

    The login

    01 UNDERSTAND

    Understanding Phishing Attacks

    A stolen password is still the most common way an account is lost. The message borrows a bank’s colours, copies a login screen and adds a deadline — but the address is never quite right. AI has made the writing cleaner; it has not learned your bank’s real domain.

    02 SEE

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. The domain is a lookalike — one swapped letter, a hyphen, or .net instead of .com.my.
    2. A deadline measured in minutes, not days.
    3. You are asked to log in from a link, instead of opening the app you already have.

    Specimen analysis / 02

    Why is this email suspicious?

    1. Unverified sender

      A familiar sender name does not prove that the email came from a genuine organization.

    2. Artificial urgency

      A 30-minute deadline pressures you to act before checking the facts.

    3. Suspicious link

      The email directs you to a website that has not been independently verified.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Type the address yourself. Never log in from a link.

  3. 03

    The voice

    01 UNDERSTAND

    AI impersonation

    Cloning a voice takes a few seconds of audio — a story, a voicemail greeting, a video. Pair it with a new number and a family emergency, and the scam becomes a conversation you believe you are having with someone you trust. Video calls are the same trick with a face.

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. A new number claims to be someone you know.
    2. The call cannot be returned, or the caller refuses to be seen.
    3. Money, gift cards or a transfer — urgently, and “don’t tell anyone yet”.

    Specimen analysis / 03

    Can you trust a familiar voice?

    Not necessarily. AI-generated voices can imitate someone you know. Pay attention to these warning signs:

    • An unfamiliar number

      The caller claims to be someone you know, but contacts you from a different number.

    • An urgent request

      They pressure you to send money, share a verification code, or act immediately.

    • Verify independently

      Contact the person using a number or channel you already trust. Never share a one-time verification code.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Hang up. Call back on the number you already have.

    05 CONTINUE

    Use the game to practise checking urgent messages that appear to come from someone you trust.

  4. 04

    The offer

    01 UNDERSTAND

    Fake Offers and Investments

    The offer arrives on WhatsApp or Instagram: flexible hours, RM300 a day, no experience needed. The work is liking videos or “verifying orders”. Then the job asks you to pay a fee, or to receive money for someone else. That is the point at which it stops being a job.

    02 SEE

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. You are asked to pay to start work — a deposit, a training fee, an “activation”.
    2. The employer exists only on a messaging app, with no company name, address or contract.
    3. Your bank account is used to move someone else’s money. That makes you the mule.

    Specimen analysis / 04

    Why is this job offer suspicious?

    A convincing recruitment message can still contain warning signs. Look closely at the claims and requests.

    1. Unverified earnings

      High earnings promised for simple tasks should be independently verified.

    2. Upfront payment

      A request to pay before starting work is a major warning sign.

    3. Artificial urgency

      Pressure to apply immediately can prevent you from checking whether the job is genuine.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Never pay to start a job. Never lend your account to move money.

    05 CONTINUE

    Test your judgment on job and investment offers before responding or sending money.

  5. 05

    The manipulated video

    01 UNDERSTAND

    Deepfake Videos

    Deepfake videos use artificial intelligence to make someone appear to say or do things they never actually said or did. Scammers may use these videos to spread fake giveaways, investment claims, or misleading news.

    02 SEE

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. A video promises unbelievable rewards or guaranteed results.
    2. The face, lips, or voice may have unnatural movements.
    3. The video creates urgency and pushes you to an unverified website.

    Specimen analysis / 05

    What makes this video suspicious?

    A realistic-looking video is not proof that its claims are genuine. Look for these warning signs:

    1. Unverified claim

      Exciting prizes or guaranteed results should be checked against an official, trusted source.

    2. Visual or audio clues

      Unnatural facial movements or mismatched audio can be warning signs, but a video without glitches is not necessarily genuine.

    3. Pressure to act

      A suspicious video may push you to click a link, enter your details, or act before verifying it.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Verify the claim through the person's official account or another trusted source before taking action.

    05 CONTINUE

    Practice questioning convincing-looking videos and checking claims through trusted sources.

  6. 06

    The protection

    01 UNDERSTAND

    Protect Yourself

    Staying safe online starts with simple habits. Pause before responding to urgent messages, verify unexpected requests independently, and keep your personal information secure.

    02 SEE

    03 RECOGNIZE

    1. Be cautious of unexpected login alerts or security warnings.
    2. Never share passwords or one-time security codes with anyone.
    3. Watch for urgent requests for money or unfamiliar links.

    04 REMEMBER

    The check Pause, verify through an official channel, and protect your accounts and personal details.
