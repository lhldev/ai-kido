Privacy policy

What this site does with your data.

AI-kido is a static workshop site. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to buy and nothing to log in to. This page explains the small amount of technical data that hosting involves, and what to do if you have a question.

Who we are

AI-kido is a free scam-awareness workshop for secondary school students in Malaysia, run by a community team in partnership with Universiti Malaya and participating schools. This policy covers this website only. It does not cover other sites we link to.

What we do not collect

You can read every page without giving us any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, school or age. There are no accounts, sign-up forms, comment boxes, newsletters or payments on this site.

We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not build a profile of your visit. This site writes nothing to your browser’s storage: no cookies, no local storage and no fingerprinting.

What the hosting providers process

This site is a set of static files. The same files are published on two hosts: Cloudflare Pages and Netlify. Whichever address you used, the host that delivered the page processes the technical data needed to serve it and keep the service secure.

That can include your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, your browser’s user-agent string and the page you came from. It may also appear in security and operational logs. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

The hosts process this data under their own privacy policies, which also explain where it is stored and for how long:

  • Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Netlify privacy statement

Both are global companies, so this technical data may be processed outside Malaysia. Their policies describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

Cookies and third parties

This site sets no cookies and includes no third-party content. The typefaces are served from this domain, and there are no embedded videos, maps, fonts, scripts or social widgets. The only outside party involved in loading a page is the host described above, which necessarily receives the request.

Some links lead to other sites — for example Semak Mule or the font licences — and those sites have their own privacy policies once you follow the link.

If you email us

If you write to hoonlimlee+privacy@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation if we need to. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your message for marketing.

The mailbox is run by Gmail, so Google processes the message as our email provider. We keep emails only for as long as needed to deal with the question. The address includes +privacy so we can tell messages about this site apart from ordinary mail.

Please do not send passwords, bank details, identity documents or anyone else’s personal information. If you are a student, ask a teacher or parent before sending us personal details.

Children and students

The site is written for secondary school students. It does not create accounts or collect profiles, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only way a student can send us personal data is by choosing to email us. A parent, guardian or teacher can write on a student’s behalf.

Your choices and rights

Under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have rights over personal data held about you, including access, correction and ending processing that depends on your consent. This site holds almost nothing: the only personal data we might have is an email you chose to send.

To ask what we hold, correct it or ask us to delete it, write to the address below. If your question is about hosting logs, the provider named above is the right contact, because those logs are held by them. You can also contact the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia if you are not satisfied with our response.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts using a different host, an analytics service or any feature that changes this picture, we will update this page and the date at the top. The colophon on the home page also records the current hosting.
